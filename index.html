FAQ

Common Questions

Can you work remotely with my engineering team?

Yes. Project requirements, drawings and revisions can be coordinated online through email, WhatsApp or your preferred communication method.

What files can I provide?

You can start with sketches, PDFs, images, dimensions, existing CAD files or reference drawings. Final formats can be agreed before the work starts.

Do you accept small CAD jobs?

Yes. The service is designed for individual drawings, revisions, components, moulds as well as larger ongoing assignments.

How is confidentiality handled?

Project information should be shared only through an agreed secure channel. Confidential or proprietary material can be handled according to the terms agreed with the client.

How do you quote a project?

Quotes can be based on scope, drawing complexity, number of parts/drawings, revisions and delivery requirements. A clear scope is confirmed before work begins.
